🔧 Mobile Service Policy – LA Basin Coverage

📍 Travel & Appointment Terms

  • Base House Call (within 40 min drive): $60
  • Extended Travel (past 40 min one way): +$30/hour (each way)
  • Max Travel Radius: 3 hours one way (6 hours total round trip)

    • Drive time is based on Google Maps ETA at time of departure. No jobs are booked if drive time exceeds limit or payout falls below service minimums.

💵 Service Minimum

  • Minimum Job Total (including travel): $150
  • If the job does not meet the minimum, it will not be booked.
  • Labor rate for major work (e.g., conversions, field soldering): $100/hr

❌ Services Not Offered Alone

These are only available when bundled with other services to meet the minimum:

  • Flat Repair (Non-Motor Side) – $20
  • Seat Installation – $20
  • Handlebar Accessory Install – $20
  • Crank Arm Installation – $20
  • Bolt Extraction – $20
  • Chain Install – $25
  • Brake Caliper Adjustment – $20
  • Derailleur Hanger Alignment – $20

These are fast, tool-specific tasks that don’t justify travel or scheduling on their own.

📱 How You Use the Minimum Service Policy:

When they ask for a small job, reply with this:

“Thanks for reaching out. I have a minimum job total of $150 including travel, so smaller services like [insert request] are only available when combined with a larger job. Let me know what else you might need, and I’ll get you booked.”

🧠 Why This Exists

  1. I run a solo field business — I’m the mechanic, the driver, and the scheduler.
  2. This policy ensures I don’t pay out of pocket to help others.
